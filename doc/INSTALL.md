# Installing Pitchoune Timer (Android)

Pitchoune Timer is not on the Play Store: it is shared with family and
friends through **Firebase App Distribution**. You receive an invitation by
email, and every new version is announced the same way.

## 1. Install the app

On the Android tablet or phone:

1. Open the invitation email **on that device** and tap **Get started**.
   Sign in with the Google account the invitation was sent to.
   - On a **tablet**, if the page says to open the email on a mobile device:
     Chrome is showing the *desktop site* (its default on large screens). In
     Chrome, menu **⋮** → untick **Desktop site**; the page reloads and
     offers the installation.
2. Follow the link to download **App Tester** (Firebase's small installer
   app), or download the app directly from the web page.
3. Android asks to allow installing apps from this source: **Settings →
   Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Install **Pitchoune Timer**. If Play Protect warns about an unknown
   developer, tap **More details → Install anyway** (the app is not on the
   Play Store, hence the warning).

## 2. First launch

- **First device of the family:** *Create a family*.
- **Other devices:** on the first device, tap the **family button** of the
  home screen: it shows a QR code. On the new device, *Join a family* →
  **Scan the QR code**, allow the camera, and point it at the first device.
  (No camera? Type the 12-character code written under the QR code.)
- Add the children (name, avatar, color) and their minutes. Changing a bank
  needs the **parent code: 728** at first; choose your own in ⚙ → *Parent
  code*, it changes on every device of the family.

As soon as the app starts, a **Setup** screen lists what Android must allow.
Tap **Allow** on each line, accept in the screen that opens, then come back:
the line gets a green ✓.
- **Display over other apps:** the end of a countdown shows up over other
  apps, and the child cannot leave a paused or ended countdown;
- **Notifications:** the time left in the status bar;
- **No battery restriction:** alarms keep working in the background. The
  button opens the app's page: tap **Battery → Unrestricted**.

The same screen is in ⚙ (parent code) → **Permissions and setup**.

## 3. A tablet with a child account (optional)

If the child has their own account on the tablet (Settings → Accounts and
backup → Users, a *restricted profile* or a *user*), the app keeps them on
their timer. In the **child's account**:

1. Make the app available: for a restricted profile, turn Pitchoune Timer on
   in the profile's allowed apps (from the owner account); for a user, install
   it from the invitation as above.
2. Open Pitchoune Timer once, join the family: scan the QR code of another
   device of the family (phone, other tablet). On a single shared tablet, the
   owner account cannot be on screen at the same time: type the code (written
   under the QR code in the owner account's family button).
3. ⚙ (parent code) → **Child** → choose the child.
4. The **Setup** screen opens: allow every line, including **Home screen of
   this account** (choose "Pitchoune Timer"). The app then opens after a
   restart and on the Home button, and the child opens their apps from it
   (*My apps*, during a countdown).

Each account keeps its own settings: the owner account stays in parent mode
with its usual home screen.

## 4. Updates

App Tester notifies new versions (also by email): tap **Download**, then
install. Settings, family, home screen and permissions are kept, in every
account of the tablet. Do **not** uninstall the app first: uninstalling erases
everything (the device would have to join the family again, and child
accounts would have to be set up again).
